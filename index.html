    About ProlificFX

    Built by traders, for traders

    ProlificFX started as a simple frustration: banking, trading, and custody tools that refused to speak to each other. We rebuilt them as one platform — so your capital, your trades, and your controls all live behind a single login.

    The team behind ProlificFX combines backgrounds in FX markets, custody infrastructure, and product engineering, with a shared focus on speed and clarity over noise.

    Security

    Security is the foundation, not a feature

    Every layer of ProlificFX is designed around the same question: what happens if something goes wrong.

    Encrypted by default

    Data is encrypted in transit and at rest across the platform.

    Multisig approvals

    Vault withdrawals require sign-off from multiple approved key holders.

    Round-the-clock monitoring

    Infrastructure and account activity are monitored continuously.

    Granular access control

    Set roles and permissions down to the individual team member.
